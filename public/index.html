Know what to ask — and how soon

Anteroom cannot tell you what you have. Nothing can, without an examination, scans and lab work. It explains what published guidelines recommend for people in your situation and what to ask. If you have chest pain, stroke signs, are struggling to breathe, are vomiting blood, have a rash that doesn't fade under pressure, or a baby under three months with a fever — stop reading and get emergency care now.

Three calculators clinicians actually use. Each one turns routine numbers into the guideline category that decides what happens next.

Everything here runs in your browser. These numbers are never sent to this site, to Google, or anywhere else. None of these is a diagnosis — they sort people into categories that guidelines act on.

FIB-4 estimates liver scarring from four numbers on routine bloods. It doesn't look for cancer — it estimates scarring, which is what decides who qualifies for liver cancer surveillance.

eGFR estimates how well the kidneys filter, using the 2021 CKD-EPI equation — the version with the race coefficient removed.

Where a reading falls in the ACC/AHA categories. One reading is not a diagnosis — hypertension is diagnosed from repeated readings, usually at home.
