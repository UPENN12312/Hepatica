Liver Cancer Research Assistant

Understand what published research says — grounded in PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov.

This is an educational tool, not a diagnostic one. It cannot tell you whether you have liver cancer, and it will not try to. It explains what the medical literature says so you can have a better-informed conversation with a doctor. If you have symptoms or a specific concern, please see a healthcare provider. For urgent symptoms — jaundice, vomiting blood, black stools, confusion, or severe abdominal pain — seek care right away.
Privacy note: this site runs on Google's free AI tier, which means what you type may be used by Google to improve their models. Please don't enter your name, contact details, or anything you wouldn't want shared. Ask in general terms where you can.
